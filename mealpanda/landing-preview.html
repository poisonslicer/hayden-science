FAQs

How does Meal Panda compare with a meal kit?

You choose and shop for your own ingredients.

Meal kits bundle recipes and ingredients into a delivery. Meal Panda helps you choose meals, create an organized grocery list, and follow the recipes in your own kitchen.

You pick your store and brands, and check what you already have before shopping.

What if planning is the part I dislike?

Start with one meal you want to eat.

Swipe through the recipes and save the ones you like. Your plan keeps the meals together, and the grocery list brings their ingredients into one place.

When you are ready to cook, open the recipe for its ingredients and instructions.

Does Meal Panda deliver groceries or place orders?

You review and shop for your groceries.

Meal Panda creates a list for you to use at your store. It does not deliver food or automatically check out a grocery order.

Do I need an account, and where is my plan saved?

You can get started as a guest.

Your guest plan and grocery checklist are saved only in this browser. They stay here when you refresh or return, but clearing this site's browser data removes them. A different browser or device has its own guest plan.

Signing in is optional. When account sync is enabled, you can sign in to sync your meal plan across devices.

How PANDAs eat

From choosing dinner to packing tomorrow’s lunch, a little planning brings the week together. Here’s how to use Meal Panda:

1

Plan

Swipe through recipes and save the meals you like. Open your plan to adjust servings or ask Rocket for a recipe edit.

2

Shop

Review your organized grocery list, check your pantry, and shop for the ingredients and brands you prefer.

3

Cook

Open a saved meal for its ingredients and cooking instructions. Check off the steps as you go.

4

Pack

If meal prep fits your week, plan portions for later and pack your meals when you’re done cooking.
